How to Calculate BMI — Complete Guide for Indians

Body Mass Index (BMI) is the most widely used screening tool to check if your weight is healthy for your height. This guide explains how to use our free BMI calculator, how to read the results, and what the Indian-specific BMI thresholds mean.

What is BMI?

BMI is calculated by dividing your weight (in kg) by the square of your height (in metres). The formula is: BMI = weight(kg) ÷ height(m)². For example, a person who weighs 70 kg and is 1.70 m tall has a BMI of 70 ÷ (1.70 × 1.70) = 24.2.

BMI Limitations

BMI does not account for muscle mass, age, or gender. A bodybuilder may show as "obese" and an elderly person with low muscle mass may appear "normal" despite being at risk. Use BMI alongside waist circumference and other health indicators for a fuller picture.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI for an Indian woman?
18.5 to 22.9 is considered normal. A BMI of 23 or above is considered overweight for South Asian women, based on ICMR guidelines.
How do I calculate BMI without a calculator?
Divide your weight (kg) by your height in metres squared. For 60 kg and 1.60 m: 60 ÷ (1.6 × 1.6) = 60 ÷ 2.56 = BMI 23.4.
Can children use this BMI calculator?
This calculator is designed for adults (18+). For children, BMI is interpreted using age- and gender-specific growth charts.
